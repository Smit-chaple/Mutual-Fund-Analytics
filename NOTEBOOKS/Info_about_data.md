- What is mfapi ?
MFapi (specifically MFapi.in) is a free, open-source REST API providing daily updated net asset value (NAV) data, scheme details, and historical records for Indian mutual funds. It requires no registration, API keys, or authentication.

- What is NAV ?
NAV stands for Net Asset Value, representing the per-unit price of a mutual fund, total assets minus liabilities, and a tool to track investment growth.

- What is AUM ?
AUM stands for Assets Under Management, representing the total market value of all financial assets a mutual fund scheme holds on behalf of its investors. It measures the overall size, popularity, and scale of a fund.
